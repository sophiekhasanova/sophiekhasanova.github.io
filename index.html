LOST is a free navigator for drivers in Ukraine, where GPS often jumps or drops out. I break down how its interface keeps people on route anyway: it estimates position from speed and time, and lets you fix it with one tap.

About me

Hi, I’m Sophia, a designer who loves turning ideas into things people can actually use.

My main focus is UI/UX and product design, but I also enjoy working with visuals, creative concepts and digital experiences. I work in Figma, explore AI-powered tools and use Claude Code to bring designs to life. I also create advertising creatives and social media content, including for LOST, a navigator app for Ukrainian drivers.

I’m always curious about new tools and new ideas. I believe good design is not just about how something looks, but also about how it works and what it makes people feel.
