How I got here

From marketing to product design, in a few steps.

  1. Where it started

    Marketing degree

    I studied marketing. It taught me to always start with people: who they are and what they need.

  2. Before 2022

    Graphic design

    Logos, posters, business cards and social media visuals. I also made fan edits of movies, shows and anime, and that’s how I fell in love with motion.

  3. 2022

    Switched to UI/UX

    I wanted to create things people don’t just look at, but actually use. Since then I’ve worked on user flows, interfaces, design systems and product case studies.

  4. Now

    Design and code

    I design in Figma, explore AI-powered tools and use Claude Code to bring my designs to life. I also create social media content for LOST, a navigator app for Ukrainian drivers.

  5. Next

    Games

    I’m drawing, writing and developing my own pixel-art horror game, Day or Night, and learning Unity along the way.

How I work

Every project is different, but this is usually how it goes.

  1. 1

    Understand

    I start with people: who they are, what they need and what gets in their way.

  2. 2

    Sketch

    Quick flows and wireframes to explore ideas before polishing anything.

  3. 3

    Design

    Clean interfaces and components in Figma, tested with real content.

  4. 4

    Build

    A working version with Claude Code or Webflow, which I test and keep improving.
